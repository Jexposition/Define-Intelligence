The probe proves that for arbitrary types, rate function, field, derivative
order, and exponent, `JetRate (⊥ : Filter D) q f m r` holds. The reason is
that `JetRate` asks only for an eventual inequality and every proposition is
eventual on the bottom filter. This is a real specification weakness. It is
not by itself a defect in the CMI result: the principal construction uses
named approach filters, and the next audit must verify their nontriviality at
every load-bearing use.
